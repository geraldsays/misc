ℹ️ About this engine
Needs no typed-in stats. It uses attack and defence ratings for every team, learned from about 27,000 matches (weighted towards recent games), with a calibration step so the percentages are honest.

Backtest (4,102 matches, 5 big leagues, unseen seasons): 52.7% right on win/draw/loss (bookmakers 54.7%), 57.4% on over/under 2.5 (bookmakers 59.1%), 55.6% on BTTS (guessing 54.8%).

Covers the teams that played in 2025/26. Teams promoted since then are not listed, and ratings do not know about injuries, lineups or transfers. Refresh the ratings after each season.
⚖️ Price Check: your bookmaker vs the sharp price
Enter your bookmaker's odds and a sharp reference (for example Pinnacle). The app removes the margin from the reference and shows how far your price is above or below fair. Log paper bets to find out, over time, whether your bookmaker is ever beatable.

📖 Method Guide
⚽ Goals-Only: Uses actual scoring. Good early season.

📊 xG-Only: Uses chance quality. Removes luck factor.

🎯 Hybrid: Blends xG and goals (finishing shown for info only). Backtest: about 49-52% correct on win/draw/loss.

🚀 Refined: Team ratings from 27,000 matches, calibrated. Most accurate engine here: about 52-53% correct on win/draw/loss.
